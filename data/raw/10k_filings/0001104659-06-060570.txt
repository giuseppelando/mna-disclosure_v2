<p style="font-family:Times New Roman;font-size:10.0pt;margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt 54.0pt;text-indent:-54.0pt;"><u><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">ITEM 1.</font></u><font size="1" style="font-size:3.0pt;">&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160; </font><u>BUSINESS</u></p>

<p style="font-family:Times New Roman;font-size:10.0pt;margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt 18.0pt;text-indent:-18.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">a.</font><font size="1" style="font-size:3.0pt;">&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160; </font>GENERAL DEVELOPMENT OF BUSINESS</p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">International Aluminum Corporation is an integrated
building products manufacturer of diversified lines of quality aluminum and
vinyl products.&#160; The Company was
incorporated in California in 1963 as successor to an aluminum fabricating
business begun in 1957 and maintains its executive offices at 767 Monterey Pass
Road, Monterey Park, California 91754.&#160;
The Company&#146;s telephone number is (323) 264-1670.&#160; Reference to the &#147;Registrant&#148;, &#147;International
Aluminum Corporation&#148; or the &#147;Company&#148; includes International Aluminum
Corporation and its subsidiaries unless the context indicates otherwise.</font></p>

<p style="font-family:Times New Roman;font-size:10.0pt;margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt 18.0pt;text-indent:-18.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">b.</font><font size="1" style="font-size:3.0pt;">&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160; </font>FINANCIAL INFORMATION ABOUT SEGMENTS</p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;page-break-after:avoid;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">This information is included
on pages 4 and 24 of the Registrant&#146;s 2006 Annual Report to Shareholders and is
hereby incorporated by reference.</font></p>

<p style="font-family:Times New Roman;font-size:10.0pt;margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt 20.0pt;text-indent:-20.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">c.</font><font size="1" style="font-size:3.0pt;">&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160;&#160; </font>NARRATIVE
DESCRIPTION OF BUSINESS</p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;"><u><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">Processes and Products</font></u></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt 20.0pt;"><u><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">Residential</font></u></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">Residential products are fabricated from aluminum
and vinyl into a broad line of horizontal sliding windows, vertical sliding
windows, casement windows, garden windows, bay and bow windows, special
configuration windows, louvre windows, patio doors and related products.&#160; These products are used in new residential
construction and in remodeling, home improvement and replacement.</font></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt 20.0pt;"><u><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">Commercial</font></u></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">Commercial products are fabricated from aluminum
into curtainwalls, window walls, slope glazed systems, storefront framing,
entrance doors and frames and commercial operable windows for exterior
applications, including storm and blast resistant applications and
officefronts, office partitions, doors and frames for interior
applications.&#160; These products are
utilized in varying combinations to produce systems used for office and
commercial construction, remodeling and tenant improvement applications.</font></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt 20.0pt;"><u><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">Aluminum Extrusion</font></u></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">In the extrusion process, heated aluminum billets
are hydraulically forced through steel dies to produce a piece of metal of the
desired cross-sectional shape and length.&#160;
The extrusions are then cut and, when requested, anodized or painted in
a variety of finishes in the Company&#146;s anodizing and painting departments.</font></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">Aluminum extrusions produced by the Company are used
in fabricating substantially all of its other aluminum products. During fiscal
2006, approximately 48% of extrusions produced were sold to non-affiliated
customers.&#160; The Company also furnishes
design services to assist its customers in developing or better utilizing
custom extrusions.</font></p>

<p style="font-family:Times New Roman;font-size:10.0pt;margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;"><u><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">ITEM 3.</font></u>&#160; <u>LEGAL PROCEEDINGS</u></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">Klotzer, et al. v. International Windows In Time,
Inc.</font></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">International Aluminum Corporation and certain of
its subsidiaries are defendants in the above-referenced lawsuit (Case no.
FCS021196) filed in December 2002 in the Superior Court for Solano County,
California.&#160; The plaintiffs in the
lawsuit are the owners of seven homes in which the Company&#146;s Series 6200 windows
are installed.&#160; The same lawyers who
represent them in this action have also brought similar class actions against
other manufacturers of aluminum windows in California</font></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">In their Third Amended Complaint filed in August
2005, the plaintiffs assert various causes of action, including strict product
liability, breach of warranty, and violation of the California Consumer Legal
Remedies Act.&#160; The plaintiffs also
purport to represent a statewide class of persons who own buildings in
California that contain the Company&#146;s Series 6200 horizontal sliding, vertical
hung, or fixed aluminum windows manufactured during the period 1993 to the
present.&#160; In November 2005, the Court
certified the plaintiff class.</font></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">According to plaintiffs, the essence of their claims
is that the Series 6200 windows leak at the lower corners.&#160; Plaintiffs contend that these leaks are
caused by the design and manufacture of the lower corners and voids and gaps in
the sealant used in the window corners; and that the leaking windows have
damaged parts of the homes in which they were installed.&#160; Plaintiffs also claim that the windows do not
perform as warranted and do not perform in compliance with American
Architectural Manufacturer&#146;s Association, or AAMA, standards.&#160; The Company denies all of these allegations.</font></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">There are approximately 80,000 owners of the Series
6200 windows in the class.&#160; The named
plaintiffs seek actual and punitive damages, as well as injunctive and
restitutionary relief on their claims, including the cost to remove and replace
all of the Series 6200 windows in the class.&#160;
The Company denies any liability.</font></p>

<p style="margin:0pt 0pt 12.0pt;text-indent:36.0pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">The Company&#146;s insurers have accepted the defense of
this lawsuit under reservation of rights.&#160;
The scope of the Company&#146;s insurance coverage may depend upon the
ultimate disposition of the plaintiffs&#146; claims.</font></p>
